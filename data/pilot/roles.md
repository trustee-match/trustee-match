# Pilot seed: six invented charities, six board vacancies

Fictional charities, written to read the way a real board vacancy ad does — specific,
a bit uneven in tone, with a genuine reason behind the need. None of these are real
organisations; names are chosen not to collide with real Guernsey charities. This is
seed content for the internal sprint demo, not public-facing listings (see
`planning/responsible-design-decisions.md`, Accuracy — these replace the real
volunteer.gg listings originally planned for this batch, for reasons logged there).

---

## 1. Longfield Foodbank — Treasurer (the financial-concern one)

**Cause:** Poverty & hardship
**Hours:** 7/month · **Meeting pattern:** Monthly board meeting, plus extra time in the first three months for the back-year catch-up
**Induction:** No formal handover — the outgoing treasurer left without one. The bookkeeper (part-time, 1 day/week) can walk you through what records exist.

We're going to be straightforward about this one: our books have fallen behind. Our treasurer of six years resigned suddenly in May for personal reasons, and nobody on the board has the background to pick it up properly. We've been getting by on a part-time bookkeeper doing basic entries, but there's been no management accounts since February, no bank reconciliation review since the handover gap started, and we genuinely don't know our real reserve position right now. We serve about 140 households a month and our biggest funder asks for audited accounts every year — that's due in four months and we are not ready.

We need someone who can get into the numbers properly: reconcile what's there, work out what's missing, and then keep monthly management accounts going forward so the board actually knows where it stands. This isn't a light-touch treasurer role for year one — expect real hours up front.

**Skills needed:** Finance & treasury (essential — must be comfortable doing hands-on reconciliation work, not just reviewing someone else's figures). Audit & risk (would help, not essential — we haven't had an external review in over two years).

## 2. Bailiwick Young Carers — Trustee, Safeguarding

**Cause:** Children & young people
**Hours:** 5/month · **Meeting pattern:** Monthly evening board meeting (Tuesdays)
**Induction:** Handover sessions with the outgoing safeguarding lead before she steps down in December, plus our existing policy pack.

We support around 60 children and young people across the Bailiwick who care for a family member at home. Our safeguarding lead on the board has done the role brilliantly for eight years but is stepping down at the end of the year, and we don't currently have anyone else who could pick it up. We also know an external review of our safeguarding arrangements is likely in the next 12–18 months as part of our funding relationship with [a statutory partner], and our policies haven't had a full review since 2023.

We need a trustee who can genuinely lead on this — not just attend training and sign off a policy once a year, but actively review our procedures, support our staff on live situations when they come up, and get us inspection-ready. Given who we work with, this matters more than most of our other board gaps right now.

**Skills needed:** Safeguarding (essential). HR & people (helpful — a fair bit of this touches staff training and supervision, not just policy).

## 3. Vale Community Hub — General Trustee

**Cause:** Community & social
**Hours:** 4/month · **Meeting pattern:** Monthly board meeting, Wednesday evenings
**Induction:** Half-day induction with the chair, plus sitting in on one meeting as an observer first if you'd like.

Honestly, this one isn't about a specific skills gap — we're not short of any one professional background right now. We run a community space used by about a dozen local groups (toddler group, a lunch club for older residents, a repair café once a month, among others), and we're a small, close board of five who are stretched thin covering everything from room bookings to grant reporting. We just need another steady pair of hands: someone who'll turn up, read the papers, ask sensible questions, and help us think things through as we grow.

We'd love this to be someone's first trustee role. We're a friendly, low-drama board and happy to show you the ropes.

**Skills needed:** None essential. Community engagement would be a nice-to-have, but mostly we're looking for judgement and reliability over a specific CV line.

## 4. Petit Bot Wildlife Rescue — Trustee, Marketing & Communications

**Cause:** Animal welfare
**Hours:** 5/month · **Meeting pattern:** Monthly board meeting, plus occasional ad hoc input between meetings (a few emails, nothing heavy)
**Induction:** Walkthrough of our current channels (a Facebook page, mostly) and what little brand material exists.

We rescue and rehabilitate injured and orphaned wildlife across the island — around 300 animals a year, mostly birds and hedgehogs, some seal pups in season. The problem is almost nobody outside our existing supporters knows we exist, and our funding is almost entirely small individual donations that have been flat for three years while our vet bills haven't been. We don't have a website worth the name, our social media is inconsistent, and we've never had anyone on the board who actually knows what they're doing on the comms side.

We need a trustee who can help us build an actual plan — social media that doesn't go quiet for six weeks at a time, some local press relationships, maybe a proper rebrand at some point — and grow our donor base before our reserves get uncomfortably thin.

**Skills needed:** Marketing & communications (essential). Fundraising (helpful — the two are closely linked for us, though we're treating them as separate gaps on our board right now).

## 5. Clos du Parc Day Centre — Trustee, Fundraising

**Cause:** Older people
**Hours:** 6/month · **Meeting pattern:** Monthly board meeting, plus a termly fundraising-strategy session
**Induction:** Session with our centre manager to understand the service, plus access to what grant records we do have.

We run a day centre for older islanders who'd otherwise be at real risk of isolation — about 45 regular attendees, hot meals, activities, transport for those who can't get here themselves. A grant that's covered roughly half our running costs for the last six years ends next March, and the funder has told us clearly it won't be renewed. We don't currently have anyone on the board with real fundraising or bid-writing experience, and our fundraising to date has basically been "the manager applies to whoever she's heard of." That's not going to replace this gap.

We need someone who can help us build an actual diversified income strategy — trusts and foundations, maybe some corporate partnerships, possibly a legacy-giving conversation eventually — starting now, because March isn't very far away and closing days or cutting transport are the realistic alternatives if we don't fix this.

**Skills needed:** Fundraising (essential, ideally including bid-writing experience). Strategy (helpful — this is as much a planning problem as a fundraising one).

## 6. Island Heritage Archive Trust — Trustee, Digital & Data

**Cause:** Arts & heritage
**Hours:** 4/month · **Meeting pattern:** Monthly board meeting, plus occasional project time for the digitisation work itself (flexible, self-directed)
**Induction:** A proper look through what we've actually got — most of it's never been catalogued — plus our (thin) existing data protection notes.

We hold a small but genuinely important collection of island photographs, oral histories and documents going back over a century, mostly donated by families over the years. Nearly all of it is still on paper or in boxes, and what little is digital lives on one volunteer's personal laptop with no backup anyone else can get to. We know this is a real risk — if that laptop dies, or that volunteer can no longer help, that's it. We also haven't properly thought through data protection for the oral history recordings, some of which include living people talking about sensitive family history.

We need a trustee who can help us get this onto a proper footing: a real digitisation plan, decent backup and storage, and a sensible approach to who can access what and why. This is as much a "stop something bad happening" project as a "nice to have."

**Skills needed:** Digital & data (essential). Project management (helpful — this is a multi-year undertaking that needs actual planning, not just good intentions).

## 7. New Routes Trust — General Trustee

**Cause:** Employment & training
**Hours:** 6/month · **Meeting pattern:** Monthly board meeting, plus a site visit to one of the three shops on rotation
**Induction:** A tour of all three sites, time with each shop manager, and a walkthrough of our current HR paperwork (such as it is).

We run three charity shops — Bridge, Town, and our HQ in an old farmhouse out in the Forest — and use the proceeds to fund work-placement and life-skills training for islanders who've struggled to find steady work. We've grown faster than our board has: we now employ nine retail staff across the three shops plus two part-time custodians looking after the properties (the farmhouse especially needs a fair bit of upkeep), and right now nobody on the board has any real HR background. We've been managing rotas, a couple of difficult performance conversations, and recruitment on instinct rather than anything resembling good practice, and we know that's not sustainable as we keep growing.

This is a general trustee role, not a specialist one — we need someone who can get stuck into the full range of what the board does. But if you've got HR or people-management experience, we'd be lying if we said that wasn't exactly what we're missing right now.

**Skills needed:** None essential — this is a genuine general trustee vacancy. HR & people would be very welcome (we have real, current gaps here), and property & estates is a smaller nice-to-have given we're responsible for three physical sites, one of them an ageing farmhouse.

## 8. Rocquaine Youth Sailing Trust — Treasurer & Company Secretary

**Cause:** Sport & recreation
**Hours:** 6/month · **Meeting pattern:** Monthly board meeting, plus the AGM and year-end accounts period
**Induction:** Full handover from our outgoing officer, including the insurance renewal cycle and the grant terms we're currently under.

We teach dinghy sailing to islanders aged 8–17, and we've grown a lot faster than our paperwork has. Five years ago we were three volunteers and two boats; now we're twelve boats, proper RYA-recognised instruction, and a grant that part-funds our safety equipment on the condition we submit audited year-end accounts and keep a compliant constitution and AGM record. One volunteer has been doing the treasurer work *and* the company-secretary work — minutes, filings, the constitution — entirely on her own for three years, and she's stepping back at the end of this season. She's asked us directly not to simply split the role in two and hope two different people stay engaged; she thinks it needs to be one person who can hold both halves together, the way she has.

So this is genuinely a two-in-one role, and we need someone who can do both properly, not one and a half. If you've only ever done the numbers, or only ever done the governance paperwork, this probably isn't the one — but if you've done both before, we'd love to hear from you.

**Skills needed:** Finance & treasury (essential) **and** Governance & company secretarial (essential) — both, not either.
